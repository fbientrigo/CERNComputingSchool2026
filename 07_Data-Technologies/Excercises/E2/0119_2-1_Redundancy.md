## Summary 2.1.3

QR codes are **Reed-Solomon (RS) codes**! The QR code used here can restore 30% of the information!

RS codes are used in storage systems to make contents on harddisks (even tape storage) redundant. They are also used in other context like communication or BlockChain technology. Mathematically a QR code contains the result vector of a matrix multiplication. We will explore now a much simpler way to get a grip how they work.
